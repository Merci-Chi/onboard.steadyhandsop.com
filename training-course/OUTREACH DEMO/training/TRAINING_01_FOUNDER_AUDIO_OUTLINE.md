# Training 01 — Founder Welcome Audio Outline

**Purpose:** A personal introduction to Steady Hands LLC and what the company stands for. This is a selection checklist, **not a script**. Check only the talking points you want included in the final audio. Leave anything you do not want unchecked.

**Suggested recording length:** 2–4 minutes. Speak naturally, in your own voice.

## 1. Welcome and personal introduction
- [ ] Welcome new sales associates to Steady Hands
- [ ] Introduce yourself as the founder
- [ ] Explain briefly why you decided to start the company
- [ ] Thank associates for being part of the company

## 2. Why Steady Hands exists
- [ ] Help small businesses afford professional websites
- [ ] Offer straightforward, accessible prices without unnecessary expense
- [ ] Make getting a website less confusing or intimidating
- [ ] Help businesses establish a credible online presence
- [ ] Make quality service accessible to businesses with smaller budgets
- [ ] Aim to provide better value and service than typical alternatives **without claiming verified lowest prices**

## 3. What we stand for
- [ ] Honesty: explain prices, services and limitations clearly
- [ ] Transparency: avoid surprise fees or misleading promises
- [ ] Helpfulness: listen to what each business actually needs
- [ ] Respect: treat every prospective customer professionally
- [ ] Quality: take pride in the websites and services we deliver
- [ ] Reliability: follow through on what we promise
- [ ] Improvement: keep finding ways to do things better

## 4. The experience we want customers to have
- [ ] Make it easy to ask questions and get understandable answers
- [ ] Be patient with owners who are unfamiliar with websites
- [ ] Focus on solving problems rather than pressuring people
- [ ] Be clear about next steps after a preview or purchase
- [ ] Offer continued support after the initial sale

## 5. What Steady Hands offers (brief overview only)
- [ ] Affordable website development
- [ ] Standard and backend website hosting
- [ ] Business email addresses
- [ ] Printed business cards
- [ ] Preview-first approach so prospects can see a possible website before deciding
- [ ] Direct associates to the Training Guide for exact current prices and plan details

## 6. What we expect from sales associates
- [ ] Represent Steady Hands accurately and honestly
- [ ] Learn the products before speaking to prospects
- [ ] Never invent features, guarantees or discounts
- [ ] Treat prospects kindly, even when they say no
- [ ] Ask questions and seek help when uncertain
- [ ] Remember that a good customer experience matters more than pressure tactics

## 7. Closing message
- [ ] Share your long-term vision for Steady Hands
- [ ] Explain that the team is helping shape the company's reputation
- [ ] Encourage associates to take their training seriously
- [ ] Thank them and introduce the next lesson: Using Outreach

---

## Recording preparation
- [ ] Select the points above that belong in the audio
- [ ] Put the selected topics in your preferred order
- [ ] Write only a few personal notes, not a word-for-word script
- [ ] Record in a quiet space
- [ ] Review for clarity, tone and accuracy
- [ ] Save final audio as `training1-welcome.mp3`
- [ ] Link the audio to Training 01 in Outreach

**Not part of this audio:** Detailed pricing, the full sales pitch, call controls, client-portal walkthrough and commission rules. Those have their own training resources.
