Commissions & Expectations

Steady Hands LLC | Sales Associate Training Guide

01. Understanding qualified sales

For this training, a completed qualifying sale occurs when the client follows the approved purchasing process and the client's payment successfully processes. An interested prospect, accepted quote or sent website preview by itself is not a commission-eligible sale.

The commission amount in this training is $30 per qualifying sale. Your signed commission agreement and current approved policy determine your actual compensation terms.

How commissions become eligible

  • Customer completes the approved purchasing steps.
  • Client payment successfully processes.
  • The commission is eligible to be collected or added to the associate's paycheck through the approved process.
  • Once disbursed to the associate, the commission can be marked Earned / Complete.

03. Refunds, cancellations & chargebacks

Refunds, cancellations and chargebacks must be handled under the signed commission agreement and current management policy. Do not assume an automatic repayment or deduction rule. Report disputes promptly and maintain accurate sales notes.

04. Professional expectations

  • Be honest. Describe services, features and pricing accurately. Do not promise unapproved guarantees.
  • Keep records updated. Record notes, call outcomes, callbacks and the correct pipeline stage.
  • Respect customers. Honor their preferences and decisions; avoid pressure.
  • Follow calling guidelines. Check the prospect's local time and comply with relevant calling laws and approved company rules.
  • Know when to escalate. Contact management about disputes, questionable payouts or policies you cannot verify.
  • Track work accurately. Activity expectations and targets must come from the current approved company policy.

06. Frequently Asked Questions

Does an unpaid client mean I earned a commission?

No. Under the training rule, the client payment must successfully process before the sale becomes commission-eligible.

Can I collect the commission or have it added to my paycheck?

Once eligible, the commission can be collected or included in a paycheck, subject to the applicable agreement and approved payout process.

When will I be paid?

No universal payout date is specified in this guide. The signed agreement and the company's current approved payout procedure determine timing.

What happens if a customer requests a refund or there is a chargeback?

Follow the signed commission agreement and management's current refund, cancellation, and chargeback procedures. Do not assume an automatic deduction or repayment rule.

How do I report a missing commission?

Record the customer/business, payment date if known, expected amount, and relevant sales notes. Contact the appropriate manager for review.

What if I am unsure of a product or price?

Check the current approved price guide or ask management before promising anything to a prospect.
